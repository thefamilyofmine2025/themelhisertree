BIOGRAPHY: Hooker, John Marshall, 83, passed away on December 5, 2020. He spent his final days surrounded by his loving family. John was born on July 2, 1937 in Nashville, Tennessee.

BIOGRAPHY: John was preceded in death by his parents, Wallace and Jessica Hooker, and his brother, James ?Jim? Hooker.

BIOGRAPHY: John graduated from Isaac Litton high School in Nashville, TN in 1955. He went on to Vanderbilt University where he obtained a degree in Electrical Engineering in 1959. He also obtained a Masters Degree in Engineering Physics from Western Kentucky University in 1969.

BIOGRAPHY: Following graduation from Vanderbilt, he went to work for General Electric as an Engineer. He worked for GE for 39 years with work assignments in Owensboro, KY, Roanoke, VA, Lynchburg, VA, Syracuse, NY, Danville, IL, Louisville, KY and Fort Wayne, IN. He retired from GE (Fort Wayne) in 1998 and moved with his wife Carol back to Louisville in 1999 to be close to family. Following his first few years working as an engineer he moved into engineering management, and received GE?s highest award for outstanding technical achievement (The Steinmetz Award) in 1993. Throughout his career and relocations he and Carol made many life-long friends who made them feel welcome at each city they lived in. For this they are grateful to those friends. John wishes to thank Carol for being such a supportive wife. Her sacrifices made it possible for him to relocate and make career advancements along the way, and to enjoy his passion for playing golf.

BIOGRAPHY: John was an avid sports fan and golfer. Some even accused him of being a ?sports nut?. Having played baseball and basketball in high school he took up golf as an outlet for his competitive urges when those playing days were over. For many years he played in numerous golf tournaments and had several trophies on his shelves to show for it. He was a member of Persimmon Ridge Golf Club for many years. As he got older his golf skills eroded, but he continued to play up until August of 2020. He was proud to ?shoot his age? many times after reaching the age of 75! He was also an avid University of Louisville sports fan, with he and Carol having season tickets to both football and basketball games. He had many fond memories of ?tailgating? and pre-game meals with wonderful friends prior to those games.

BIOGRAPHY: John was a member of Hurstbourne Baptist Church, where he and Carol were active members of their Sunday School Class. Many wonderful friends were made through that class. He also served as a Deacon, and chaired several church committees. He and Carol had previously been members of Baptist churches in each city they lived in over the years. He was a devoted Christian and his love for his Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ, grew over the years with much help from his church friends, pastors, and most of all from his wife Carol.

BIOGRAPHY: John is survived by his wife Carol, his son, John Kenneth Hooker (Nannette), his daughter, Janice Hooker Fetzer (Keith), and grandchildren John Hooker, Lauren Fetzer, Joseph Hooker, and Luke Fetzer. His children and grandchildren were a great source of joy for him. He was proud of them all. He also is survived by step-grandchildren Erika Edwards, Alexandria Harbin (Alex), and Clayton Edwards, and step great-grandchild Norah. He adored all of his family, those survivors listed as well as a very large extended family. He enjoyed spending time with them and hoped that in some small way he was a role model and inspiration to all of them.

BIOGRAPHY: Out of respect and concern for public health and safety, all services will be private.

BIOGRAPHY: Ratterman Funeral Home, 12900 Shelbyville Road, East Louisville was entrusted with arrangements.
